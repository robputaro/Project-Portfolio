§03

Approach

Closer to product thinking than traditional creative.

I usually enter through a creative, sales, or operational problem — and end up building the system underneath it.

The output might be a diagnostic, calculator, internal workflow, presentation system, client copilot, or data product. The through-line stays the same: make abstract strategy tangible enough to use.

  1. 01
    Understand the operator
    Start from the person who has to run it, not the org chart.
  2. 02
    Simplify the decision
    Find the one call the tool exists to make easier.
  3. 03
    Design the interface
    Shape the surface before the feature list grows.
  4. 04
    Wire up the logic
    Scoring, routing, math — the part that actually decides.
  5. 05
    Ship something usable
    Put a working version in someone's hands, fast.
